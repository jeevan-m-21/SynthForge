VALUE PROPOSITION

Why Synthetic Data?

Real datasets often contain sensitive or regulated information that cannot be shared freely. Synthetic data provides a practical bridge for teams requiring realistic tabular data.

🧪

Safe Experimentation & Prototyping

Develop UI mockups, test data pipelines, and prototype analytics systems without requesting access to sensitive, restricted databases.

🤖

Machine Learning Development

Train models on synthetic datasets and benchmark generalizability using Train on Synthetic, Test on Real (TSTR) accuracy parity.

🤝

Cross-Team Collaboration

Share representative tabular datasets across engineering teams, external audit vendors, and research partners with confidence.

🛡️

Empirical Privacy Threat Defense

Stress-test generated records against membership inference, re-identification, and attribute disclosure attacks to prevent data leakage.
